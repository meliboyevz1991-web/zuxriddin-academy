Kimdir qanday savol berganini boshqalarga yetkazishda gap oddiy darak so‘z tartibiga o‘tadi. Ha/yo‘q savollarida if yoki whether ishlatiladi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Suhbat va intervyuni keyin boshqa odamga aytib berish.

ASOSIY FORMULAasked + (object) + if/whether + subject + verb; asked + wh-word + subject + verb.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjima
'Where do you live?' → She asked where I lived.U qayerda yashashimni so‘radi.
'Are you ready?' → He asked if I was ready.U tayyormisiz, deb so‘radi.
'What time is it?' → She asked what time it was.U soat nechaligini so‘radi.
💡 Esda tuting: Bilvosita savolda do/does/did yordamchi fe’li saqlanmaydi: asked where I lived.
