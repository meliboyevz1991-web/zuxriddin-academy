The Community Garden

Read the story about a new garden and answer five questions.

🎓 A2📝 5 questions📊 Automatic scoring

📖 Reading passage

Last spring, a group of neighbours in Green Street decided to make a small garden. There was an empty piece of land between the library and a supermarket. For years, nobody had used it.

Every Saturday morning, the neighbours met there. Some people brought tools, while others carried water or planted flowers. Twelve-year-old Sam helped his grandmother grow tomatoes and beans. At first, he thought gardening would be boring, but he soon enjoyed watching the plants change.

In June, the local school visited the garden. The students painted signs showing the names of different plants. They also built a small house for insects. After that, the garden became a popular place for families.

Now the neighbours share the vegetables they grow. They do not sell them. Sam says his favourite part is meeting people he did not know before. Next year, the group hopes to add a bench so older visitors can rest.
