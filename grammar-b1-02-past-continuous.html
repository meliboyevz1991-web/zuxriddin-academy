Past Continuous

Past Continuous ma’lum bir o‘tgan paytda davom etayotgan harakatni bildiradi. Uni qisqaroq sodir bo‘lgan voqea bilan birga ishlatish mumkin.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

When bilan ko‘pincha harakatni to‘xtatgan hodisa; while bilan davom etayotgan jarayon tasvirlanadi.

ASOSIY FORMULAwas / were + V-ing; When + Past Simple, Past Continuous; While + Past Continuous, Past Simple.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
I was reading when my phone rang.Telefon jiringlaganda kitob o‘qiyotgan edim.
They were playing football at six.Ular soat oltida futbol o‘ynayotgan edi.
While I was walking, it started to rain.Yurayotganimda yomg‘ir boshlandi.
💡 Esda tuting: Was bilan he/she/I, were bilan you/we/they ishlatiladi.
