Future Perfect & Future Continuous

Future Continuous kelajakdagi aniq bir vaqtda jarayon davom etishini; Future Perfect esa kelajakdagi bir nuqtaga qadar ish tugashini bildiradi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Kelajak rejasi, vaqt chegarasi va prognoz.

ASOSIY FORMULAFuture Continuous: will be + V-ing. Future Perfect: will have + V3.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjima
At 8 tomorrow, I'll be studying.Ertaga soat 8 da o‘qiyotgan bo‘laman.
By Friday, I'll have finished the project.Jumagacha loyihani tugatgan bo‘laman.
This time next year, we'll be travelling.Kelasi yil shu payt sayohat qilayotgan bo‘lamiz.
💡 Esda tuting: At 8 — odatda jarayon; by 8 — shu paytgacha yakunlangan natija.
