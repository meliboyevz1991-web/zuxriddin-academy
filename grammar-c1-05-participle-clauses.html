C1 Grammar · Dars 05 / 10

Participle Clauses

Participle clauses uzun ergash gapni qisqartirishga yordam beradi. -ing faol jarayon, V3 passiv ma’no, having + V3 esa oldin tugagan ishni ta’kidlaydi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Akademik matn, tasvirlash va sabab-natijani ixcham ifodalash.

ASOSIY FORMULAV-ing (simultaneous); V3 (passive); Having + V3 (earlier completed action).
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
Having read the report, she made a decision.Hisobotni o‘qib bo‘lgach, qaror qildi.
Built in 1900, the house is historic.1900-yilda qurilgan uy tarixiy.
Walking through the park, I met Lina.Bog‘da yurganimda Linani uchratdim.
💡 Esda tuting: Participle clause ning yashirin egasi asosiy gap egasi bilan mos kelishi kerak.
