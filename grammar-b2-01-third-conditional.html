Third Conditional

Third Conditional o'tmishda boshqa vaziyat bo‘lganida qanday natija yuz berishi mumkinligini tasavvur qilish uchun ishlatiladi. Aslida o‘tmishni o‘zgartirib bo‘lmaydi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Afsuslanish, o‘tmishdagi xatodan xulosa chiqarish va tasavvuriy natija.

ASOSIY FORMULAIf + had + V3, would / could / might have + V3.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjima
If I'd left earlier, I would have caught the bus.Oldinroq chiqqanimda, avtobusga ulgurgan bo‘lardim.
If she had studied, she would have passed.O‘qiganida, imtihondan o‘tgan bo‘lardi.
We could have gone if it hadn't rained.Yomg‘ir yog‘maganida, borgan bo‘lardik.
💡 Esda tuting: If qismida would have emas, had + V3 ishlatiladi.
