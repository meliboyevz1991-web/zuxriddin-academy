B2 Grammar · Dars 09 / 10

Have / Get Something Done

Have/get something done ishni o‘zimiz emas, boshqa kishiga bajartirganimizni anglatadi. Gapda xizmat va natija muhim.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Sartarosh, ustaxona, texnik ta’mir, suratga olish va xizmatlar.

ASOSIY FORMULAhave / get + object + V3; had + object + V3 (past).
Inglizcha misollar va tarjima
I had my hair cut yesterday.Kecha sochimni oldirdim.
We're getting the car repaired.Mashinamizni tuzattiryapmiz.
She has her house cleaned every week.U har hafta uyini tozalatadi.
💡 Esda tuting: Have my hair cut — o‘zim soch olganim emas, sartaroshga oldirganim.
