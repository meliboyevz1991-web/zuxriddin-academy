Modal Perfect: Past Deduction

Modal + have + V3 o‘tmishdagi voqeaga taxmin, ishonch yoki afsus bildirish uchun qo‘llanadi: must have (deyarli aniq), can't have (bo‘lishi mumkin emas), might have (ehtimol), should have (qilish kerak edi).

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

O‘tgan hodisani tushuntirish, ehtimol aytish va yaxshi qilinmagan ishni tahlil qilish.

ASOSIY FORMULAmust / might / can't / should / needn't + have + V3.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjima
She must have forgotten.U unutgan bo‘lsa kerak (kuchli taxmin).
He can't have seen me; he was abroad.U meni ko‘rmagan bo‘lishi kerak; chet elda edi.
You should have called me.Menga telefon qilishing kerak edi.
💡 Esda tuting: Must have + V3 kuchli taxmin; should have + V3 bajarilmagan ishga tanqid.
