Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Present Perfect o‘tmishdagi tajriba yoki hozirga ta’sir qiladigan holat uchun ishlatiladi; Past Simple esa aniq, tugagan o‘tmish vaqti bilan ishlatiladi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Vaqt aniq aytilgan bo‘lsa (yesterday, last Friday), odatda Past Simple; vaqt aytilmagan tajriba yoki hozirgi natija bo‘lsa Present Perfect.

ASOSIY FORMULAhave / has + V3 (ever, never, yet, already); Past Simple: V2 (yesterday, last year, in 2020).
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
I have visited Samarkand twice.Men Samarqandga ikki marta borganman.
I visited Samarkand last summer.Men o‘tgan yozda Samarqandga bordim.
She has just finished her homework.U hozirgina uy vazifasini tugatdi.
💡 Esda tuting: I've seen him yesterday demang; I saw him yesterday deyiladi.
