C1 Grammar · Dars 03 / 10

Conditional Inversion

Rasmiy ingliz tilida if so‘zini tushirib, had, were yoki should ni gap boshiga olib chiqish mumkin. Ma’no shart gapligicha qoladi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Rasmiy shartnomalar, maqolalar va murakkab xayoliy vaziyatlar.

ASOSIY FORMULAHad + subject + V3; Were + subject + to V1; Should + subject + V1.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
Had I known, I'd have helped.Bilganimda, yordam berardim.
Were she to apply, we'd consider her.Ariza topshirsa, ko‘rib chiqardik.
Should you need help, call us.Yordam kerak bo‘lsa, qo‘ng‘iroq qiling.
💡 Esda tuting: Had I known = If I had known; Were she to apply = If she were to apply.
