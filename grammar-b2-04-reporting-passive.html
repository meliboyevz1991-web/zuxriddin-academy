Reporting Passive

It is said that... va He is believed to... kabi tuzilmalar kimningdir xabari yoki umumiy qarashini obyektiv uslubda yetkazadi. O‘tgan hodisani bildiruvchi infinitivda to have + V3 ishlatiladi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Yangiliklar, rasmiy hisobotlar va ehtiyotkor ma’lumot yetkazish.

ASOSIY FORMULAIt is said that + gap. Subject + is/was said/believed + to V1 / to have V3.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjima
It is said that she lives abroad.Aytishlaricha, u chet elda yashaydi.
She is believed to live abroad.U chet elda yashaydi, deb hisoblanadi.
He is thought to have left yesterday.U kecha ketgan, deb o‘ylashadi.
💡 Esda tuting: Xabar berilgan paytdan oldingi ish uchun to have + V3 qo‘llanadi.
