Fronting & Emphasis

C1 ingliz tilida gapning bir qismini oldinga olib chiqish orqali kuchli ta’kid qilinadi. So ... that, such ... that, only after ... kabi uslublar matnni ifodaliroq qiladi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Adabiy, rasmiy va ta’sirli nutqda muhim natijani urg‘ulash.

ASOSIY FORMULASo + adjective + auxiliary + subject + that ...; Such + auxiliary + subject + that ...; Only after + clause + auxiliary + subject.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
So loud was the music that we left.Musiqa shunchalik baland ediki, chiqib ketdik.
Such was the demand that tickets sold out.Talab shunchalik katta ediki, chiptalar tugadi.
Only then did I understand.Faqat shundagina tushundim.
