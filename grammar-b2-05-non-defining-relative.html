B2 Grammar · Dars 05 / 10

Non-defining Relative Clauses

Non-defining relative clause shaxs yoki narsa haqida qo‘shimcha ma’lumot beradi. U vergullar bilan ajratiladi va odatda who, which, whose yoki where ishlatiladi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Tinglovchi allaqachon biladigan odam/narsa haqida qo‘shimcha tafsilot berish.

ASOSIY FORMULAMy brother, who lives in Bukhara, is a doctor. Bu turda that ishlatilmaydi.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjima
My sister, who lives in Tashkent, is a teacher.Toshkentda yashaydigan opam o‘qituvchi.
This laptop, which I bought yesterday, is fast.Kecha sotib olgan noutbukim tez.
Samarkand, where my uncle lives, is beautiful.Amakim yashaydigan Samarqand chiroyli.
💡 Esda tuting: Vergul orasida qo‘shimcha ma’lumot kelganda that emas, who/which/where/whose ishlating.
