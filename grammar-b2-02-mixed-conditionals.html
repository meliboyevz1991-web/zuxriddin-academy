Mixed Conditionals

Mixed Conditionals shart va natija turli vaqtlarga tegishli bo‘lganida ishlatiladi. O‘tmishdagi sabab bugungi holatga ta’sir qilishi yoki hozirgi holat o‘tmishdagi natijani tasavvuriy o‘zgartirishi mumkin.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

O‘tmishda boshqa qaror qilganimizda bugun nima boshqacha bo‘lishini ifodalash.

ASOSIY FORMULAPast → now: If + had V3, would + V1. Now → past: If + Past Simple, would have + V3.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjima
If I'd saved money, I'd have a car now.Pul jamg‘arganimda, hozir mashinam bo‘lardi.
If she weren't so shy, she'd have spoken yesterday.U bunchalik tortinchoq bo‘lmaganida, kecha gapirgan bo‘lardi.
If I'd learned Spanish, I could work in Madrid now.Ispan tilini o‘rganganimda, hozir Madridda ishlay olardim.
💡 Esda tuting: Savol berishdan oldin sabab va natija qaysi vaqtga tegishli ekanini aniqlang.
