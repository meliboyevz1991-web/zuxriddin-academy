Mandative Subjunctive

Recommend, insist, demand, propose, essential, vital kabi so‘zlardan so‘ng that-clause ichida ingliz tilining ayrim rasmiy variantlarida fe’lning oddiy asosiy shakli ishlatiladi; he/she bilan ham -s qo‘shilmaydi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Qonun, siyosat, boshqaruv, rasmiy taklif va tavsiyalar.

ASOSIY FORMULArecommend / insist / demand that + subject + (not) V1; passive: (not) be + V3.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
They recommended that he be present.Uning qatnashishi tavsiya qilindi.
She insisted that he stop immediately.U darhol to‘xtashini qat’iy so‘radi.
It's vital that the rules be followed.Qoidalarga amal qilinishi juda muhim.
💡 Esda tuting: He is present emas, mandative subjunctive da he be present (rasmiy variant). Britan inglizchasida should + V1 ham uchraydi.
