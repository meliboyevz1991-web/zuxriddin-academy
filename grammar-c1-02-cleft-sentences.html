C1 Grammar · Dars 02 / 10

Cleft Sentences

Cleft sentence oddiy gapdagi bitta ma’lumotni alohida urg‘ulaydi. Eng ko‘p ishlatiladigan tuzilmalar It + be ... that/who va What ... be ....

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Yozma va og‘zaki nutqda vaqt, joy, odam yoki sababni alohida ajratish.

ASOSIY FORMULAIt was + ta’kidlangan bo‘lak + who/that + gap; What + gap + be + ta’kidlangan bo‘lak.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
It was Sarah who solved the problem.Muammoni aynan Sara hal qildi.
What I need is more time.Menga kerak bo‘lgani — ko‘proq vaqt.
It was in June that they met.Ular aynan iyun oyida uchrashgan.
💡 Esda tuting: It-cleft da ko‘pincha that/who; what-cleft da What + clause + be ishlatiladi.
