B1 · Dars 05 / 10

Modals: Must, Have to, Should

Must va have to majburiyatni; should maslahatni; mustn't taqiqni; don't have to esa majburiyat yo‘qligini bildiradi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Qoidalar, qonunlar, maktab talablari va kundalik maslahatlar.

ASOSIY FORMULAmust + V1; have to + V1; should + V1; mustn't = taqiqlangan; don't have to = shart emas.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
You mustn't park here.Bu yerda mashina qo‘yish taqiqlanadi.
You don't have to come early.Erta kelishingiz shart emas.
You should drink more water.Ko‘proq suv ichishingiz kerak (maslahat).
💡 Esda tuting: Mustn't — mumkin emas; don't have to — qilish shart emas.
