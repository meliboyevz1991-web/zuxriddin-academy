C1 Grammar · Dars 06 / 10

Ellipsis & Substitution

Bir xil ma’lumotni takrorlamaslik uchun so, neither, nor, if so, if not, do so kabi tuzilmalardan foydalaniladi. Yordamchi fe’l zamon va egaga mos bo‘lishi kerak.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Ravon suhbat va yozma matnda takrorni kamaytirish.

ASOSIY FORMULASo + auxiliary + subject; Neither/Nor + auxiliary + subject; I hope so / I hope not.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
I can swim, and so can she.Men suza olaman, u ham.
He hasn't arrived, and neither have I.U kelmagan, men ham.
Will it work? I hope so.Ishlaydimi? Shunday bo‘lishidan umid qilaman.
💡 Esda tuting: Positive agreement uchun so, negative agreement uchun neither/nor ishlatiladi.
