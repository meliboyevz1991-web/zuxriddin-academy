Gerunds & Infinitives

Bir fe’ldan keyin boshqa fe’l kelsa, birinchi fe’lga qarab -ing yoki to + V1 ishlatiladi. Enjoy va avoid dan keyin -ing; want va decide dan keyin to + V1.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Rejalar, yoqtirish, qarorlar va faoliyatlar.

ASOSIY FORMULAenjoy/avoid/finish/suggest + V-ing; want/decide/hope/promise/learn + to V1.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
I enjoy swimming.Men suzishni yoqtiraman.
She decided to study English.U ingliz tilini o‘rganishga qaror qildi.
He avoided eating sweets.U shirinlik yeyishdan tiyildi.
💡 Esda tuting: Enjoy to swim emas, enjoy swimming; decide studying emas, decide to study.
