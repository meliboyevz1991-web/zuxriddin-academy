C1 Grammar · Dars 07 / 10

Nominalisation in Academic Writing

Nominalisation harakat yoki sifatni ot orqali ifodalab, rasmiy hamda akademik matnni ixchamlashtiradi. Masalan, decide → decision, analyse → analysis.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Ilmiy insho, hisobot va loyiha takliflarida jarayondan ko‘ra tushunchaga urg‘u berish.

ASOSIY FORMULAdecide → decision; implement → implementation; improve → improvement.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
They decided to expand. → Their decision to expand...Ular kengayishga qaror qilishdi → kengayish qarori...
We analysed the data. → Our analysis of the data...Ma’lumotlarni tahlil qildik → ma’lumotlar tahlili...
The system improved. → An improvement in the system...Tizim yaxshilandi → tizimdagi yaxshilanish...
💡 Esda tuting: Nominalisation dan keyin mos predlogni tanlang: analysis of, improvement in, investigation into.
