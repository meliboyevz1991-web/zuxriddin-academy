Reading · A2 · Test 02

A Busy Saturday

Read the story and choose the correct answer for each question. This is an original practice test created for Zuxriddin Academy.

The Story

On Saturday morning, Maya wanted to visit the new bookshop in the town centre. She usually went shopping with her sister, but her sister was busy studying for an exam. So Maya decided to go alone.

Before leaving home, she checked the bus timetable on her phone. The next bus was in twenty minutes. She had enough time to make a sandwich and put a bottle of water in her bag.

When Maya arrived in town, she found that the bookshop did not open until eleven o'clock. It was only ten thirty. Instead of waiting outside, she went to a nearby café and read a magazine.

At eleven, Maya entered the bookshop. She looked at books about travel because she wanted to visit Italy one day. She bought a small guidebook and a notebook. On her way home, she called her sister and promised to show her the books that evening.

Questions

5 questions · 1 point each

1. Why did Maya go to town alone?
2. What did Maya do before leaving home?
3. What time did the bookshop open?
4. What did Maya do while she waited?
5. Why was Maya interested in travel books?
