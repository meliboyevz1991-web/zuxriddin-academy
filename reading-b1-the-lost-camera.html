The Lost Camera

Read the B1 story about honesty and making a difficult decision.

🎓 B1📝 5 questions📊 Automatic scoring

📖 Reading passage

When Olivia arrived at the train station, she discovered that her camera was missing. She had been taking photographs in the park that morning, so she hurried back to look for it. The camera was not especially expensive, but it contained pictures from her grandfather's eightieth birthday. Those pictures were impossible to replace.

Back at the park, Olivia checked the bench where she had eaten lunch. Nothing was there. She asked a gardener whether anyone had handed in a camera. He suggested visiting the visitor centre, which kept a box of lost property.

The centre was about to close. Olivia explained what had happened, and a member of staff brought out a black camera. It looked exactly like hers, but she knew it could belong to someone else. To prove it was hers, Olivia described the green strap and a photograph of her grandfather holding a large chocolate cake.

The staff member smiled and returned the camera. Someone had found it beside a rubbish bin and brought it to the centre. Olivia was relieved. She decided to print her favourite photographs and give one to the person who had found the camera if she ever met them.
