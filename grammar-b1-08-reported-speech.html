Reported Speech: Statements

Birovning so‘zlarini o‘z so‘zimiz bilan yetkazamiz. O‘tgan zamondagi said/told dan keyin hozirgi zamon ko‘pincha bir pog‘ona orqaga suriladi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Do‘stingiz nima deganini yoki o‘qituvchi ko‘rsatmasini hikoya qilish.

ASOSIY FORMULAsay → said; tell + object → told + object; am/is → was; will → would; can → could.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
'I'm tired.' → She said she was tired.U charchaganini aytdi.
'I will help.' → He said he would help.U yordam berishini aytdi.
'We live here.' → They said they lived there.Ular shu yerda yashashlarini aytishdi.
💡 Esda tuting: Told dan keyin kimga aytilgani keladi: told me, told him.
