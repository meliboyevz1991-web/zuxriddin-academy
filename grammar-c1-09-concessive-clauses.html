Concessive Clauses & Linkers

Although, despite, nevertheless, whereas, much as, no matter how kabi bog‘lovchilar va iboralar kutilmagan natija yoki qarama-qarshilikni aniq ifodalaydi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

C1 darajadagi argumentativ insho, akademik matn va munozarada.

ASOSIY FORMULAalthough + clause; despite + noun/V-ing; nevertheless + new sentence; no matter how + adjective/adverb + clause.
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
Despite the rain, we went out.Yomg‘irga qaramay, tashqariga chiqdik.
Although she was tired, she worked.Charchaganiga qaramay, u ishladi.
Much as I admire him, I disagree.Uni qanchalik hurmat qilsam ham, fikriga qo‘shilmayman.
💡 Esda tuting: Despite dan keyin to‘liq clause emas, ot yoki -ing; although dan keyin to‘liq clause keladi.
