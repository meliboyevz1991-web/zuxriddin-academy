C1 Grammar · Dars 08 / 10

Advanced Modal Deduction

Must have, can't have, might have, should have been, needn't have kabi tuzilmalar o‘tmish haqida turli darajadagi ishonch, tanqid va ortiqcha bajarilgan ishni ifodalaydi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Dalillarni baholash va bo‘lib o‘tgan ishlar haqida asosli xulosa chiqarish.

ASOSIY FORMULAmust/can't/might + have + V3; should + have + been + V3 (past passive).
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
He must have been tired.U juda charchagan bo‘lsa kerak.
She can't have read it; it is still sealed.U buni o‘qimagan bo‘lishi kerak; hali yopiq.
The equipment should have been checked.Uskunani tekshirish kerak edi.
💡 Esda tuting: Must have — kuchli taxmin; can't have — deyarli imkonsizlik; might have — imkoniyat.
