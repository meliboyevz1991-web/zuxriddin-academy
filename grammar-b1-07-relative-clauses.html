Defining Relative Clauses

Who odamlar; which narsalar; where joylar; whose egalikni ifodalaydi. Aniqlovchi gap aynan qaysi odam yoki narsa ekanini bildiradi.

📖 Qoidani tushunib oling

Biron shaxs yoki narsani aniqroq ta’riflash.

ASOSIY FORMULAperson + who; thing + which; place + where; person + whose + noun
Inglizcha misollar va tarjimasi
The woman who lives next door is a doctor.Qo‘shni uyda yashaydigan ayol shifokor.
This is the café where we met.Bu biz uchrashgan kafe.
A boy whose bag was lost called us.Sumkasi yo‘qolgan bola bizga qo‘ng‘iroq qildi.
💡 Esda tuting: Egalik uchun whose, joyni bildiruvchi gapda where ishlating.
